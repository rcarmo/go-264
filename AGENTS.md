## Current profiling and cleanup rule — supersedes older text below

Rui's explicit rule: **profile and tune during pre-release tests; remove profiling data immediately after analysis/use.** Ordinary development tests do not require profiling on every run. Targeted diagnostic profiling is optional when useful and follows the same disposal rule.

- During pre-release verification, capture CPU and heap/allocation behaviour, analyse hotspots and tune avoidable allocations/repeated work. Compare equivalent workloads without weakening correctness/security/numerical contracts. For Go, inspect CPU, alloc_space and alloc_objects; coverage alone is not profiling.
- Keep captures and matching artifacts only while the current analysis needs them. Once used, immediately delete raw profiles, traces, matching test binaries, temporary fixtures and disposable logs, including failed/probe artifacts after diagnosis. Retain only concise conclusions and important measurements/limitations. Do not keep indefinite raw archives or copy them into exports/reports/evidence to evade cleanup.
- Remove all completed disposable cache/build/test/run data promptly. Never delete files still in use: finish or safely pause the owning job and clean at a safe boundary. Preserve source, installed toolchains, durable datasets/checkpoints and intentional release assets. Minimise disk usage; no random exports or redundant snapshots.
- This rule overrides **every conflicting older paragraph in this file and linked local guidance**, including “profile every test”, “unprofiled tests prohibited”, “retain all raw evidence”, “never delete profiles” and cleanup exclusions based only on an evidence/profiles directory name. Update helper/CI cleanup behaviour accordingly; do not weaken pre-release analysis.
- Existing no-agent-contact and execution-pause rules remain unchanged. This policy grants no unsolicited coordination or automatic job restart.

# go-264 project rules

The canonical project name is `go-264`. `Makefile` and the vendored `scripts/project-tmp.sh` resolve the root once before child TMPDIR is set: a validated explicit `PROJECT_TMP_BASE/go-264` or absolute `PROJECT_TMP_ROOT` ending in `/go-264` (both must agree); otherwise local writable `/workspace/tmp/go-264` then `/tmp/go-264`, or in CI `${RUNNER_TEMP}/go-264`, the original `${TMPDIR}/go-264`, then `/tmp/go-264`. Invalid explicit overrides fail. Portable CI needs no host `/workspace/Makefile`. The helper snapshots the original TMPDIR before child overrides. Keep rebuildable caches in `cache/<tool>/`, generated binaries/build trees in `build/`, disposable test artifacts in `tests/`, disposable logs in `logs/`, and isolated scratch in `runs/<purpose>/<run-id>/`; never use ad-hoc top-level paths or home caches. Initialise directories with `make tmp-init` before cache-producing commands.

Make exports GOCACHE/GOMODCACHE/GOPATH, XDG/Python/uv/pip and TMPDIR/TMP/TEMP/GOTMPDIR under the resolved root; direct commands use those paths. During pre-release verification, `scripts/test-profile.sh` and the profiling-aware Make targets capture per-package CPU/heap and analyse cumulative CPU, alloc_space and alloc_objects. `/workspace/reports/go-264/tests/<run-id>/` is transient capture storage: delete profiles, matching binaries and logs after analysis, retaining concise conclusions only. Report empty/missing samples. Ordinary development tests need not be profiled; vet/cross-builds are not test execution.

Canonical fixtures remain in `/workspace/reports/go-264/fixtures/`; use GO264_FIXTURE_ROOT/GO264_CONFORMANCE_ROOT/GO264_FFMPEG_BIN/FFMPEG_SRC for existing reference inputs and tools. Do not substitute another encoding for the missing historical BBB fixture. Raw profiles and full logs under `/workspace/reports/go-264/` or GO264_EVIDENCE_ROOT are disposable immediately after analysis; retain concise conclusions and intentional release assets only. FFmpeg downloads/build scratch remain project-owned and disposable after use. Preserve installed toolchains, canonical media and active files.

The Makefile provides no blanket `clean` target. Cleanup is scoped only to this project's completed disposable `cache/`, `build/`, `tests/`, `logs/` and `runs/` files and raw captures under report paths, after checking active jobs. Preserve concise conclusions, fixtures, installed toolchains, durable datasets/checkpoints and other-project paths. Validate local and CI fallbacks and both explicit overrides with `scripts/project-tmp.sh`. Test-owned isolated filesystem roots stay under the run directory, with existing symlink and ownership checks intact.

H.264 decoder outputs require exact sample equality against an independent pinned reference. Preserve the supported progressive 8-bit YUV420 scope; do not relax correctness, cancellation or security contracts for speed. For Git, do not rebase.
